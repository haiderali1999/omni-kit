Common questions

Where is my data?

Only on your device, in the browser's extension storage. Uninstalling removes it. Use Settings → Export to keep a backup.

How do I turn off clipboard history or snippets?

Open the popup → Settings tab and switch the feature off. It applies to tabs you open or refresh afterwards.

Prayer times

Optional and off by default. Enable it in Settings, then enter your city and country in the Alerts tab.

The bug reporter captures nothing

Turn on "Bug reporter error buffering" in Settings, then reload the page you want to report.
